# Five-Person Usability Check

Use five non-technical participants. They do not need to own a business. Do not coach them after reading the opening prompt.

## Opening prompt

> “Imagine you own Juniper Coffee. You have three minutes before opening. Use this app to understand today’s best opportunity, put one move into your plan, and find where you would record the result afterward.”

## Observe

Record only:

- Time to identify the first recommendation.
- Whether they can explain which signals drove it.
- Whether they can add it to Today’s Playbook.
- Whether they can mark it completed and find result logging.
- One point of confusion stated in their own words.

## Success thresholds

- 5/5 reach an actionable recommendation without help.
- At least 4/5 correctly name two contributing signals.
- At least 4/5 add an action and find the outcome flow within two minutes.
- Median total task time is under three minutes.
